Order

It only moves on when the person on duty presses I cleaned. Miss a day and your name stays up.

Clean when it wasn't your turn and you get the next one off. Whoever was due goes again tomorrow.

Away: your name is off the list, then comes back up two or three days after your last day away, ahead of whoever was next. More than two weeks away and it's three or four days. Two of you home together take consecutive days, longest since your last turn first.

Clean for someone right before you leave and that pays for your turn back, so you rejoin a full lap later instead.

Let your name sit there seven days running and you're paying for a cleaning service.
